# FEBRACA Brand

Official repository of the visual identity of the **Federação Brasileira da Causa Animal** (Brazilian Federation for the Animal Cause). It gathers the approved versions of the visual signature in SVG, the Visual Identity Manual and the rules of use.

The repository is public so that whoever needs the brand uses the right version, the right way. **This does not mean the brand is free to use** — it is registered, belongs to FEBRACA and may only be used under the conditions of the [LICENSE](LICENSE).

---

## Before using

Three things, in order:

1. Read the [LICENSE](LICENSE) and confirm that your use is covered by it.
2. Consult the [Visual Identity Manual](manual/Manual_Da_Marca.pdf) — it is the complete reference and takes precedence over this summary.
3. Download the original file. Do not recreate the brand, do not crop it from a screenshot and do not extract it from other material.
   If your use is not covered by the license, write to **[contato@febraca.org.br]** before publishing.

## What you may do

- Reference FEBRACA in news stories, articles, research and educational material.

- Identify a real and existing relationship with FEBRACA — participation in a programme, mentorship, research, event or formalised partnership.

- Resize the signature proportionally and choose among the five approved versions.

- Redistribute the Manual, whole and unaltered.

## What you may not do

- Alter colours, typography, proportions, layout or spacing.

- Deform, rotate, crop or apply effects, shadows, outlines and gradients.

- Use the brand as your own, or as a brand element, logo, company name, domain name or social media profile.

- Suggest sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation or partnership that does not exist.

- Use the brand in electoral propaganda or political campaign material.

- Use the brand on products for sale, merchandising or fundraising without prior written authorisation.

- Associate the brand with animal mistreatment or exploitation, or with unlawful, discriminatory, defamatory or misleading content.
  The complete and binding list is in the [LICENSE](LICENSE).
